Cubalance

Press Kit & Media Factsheet

Description

Cubalance is a physics-based match-3 puzzle game where classic color-matching collides with dynamic weight distribution on seesaw scales. Players must manage physical momentum to tip scales, catapult cubes across the screen, clear handcrafted adventure puzzles, or sabotage opponents in chaotic split-screen local PvP.

Key Features

  • Physics-Driven Matching: Color matching meets dynamic gravity where every cube carries physical weight.
  • Strategic Momentum: Overload seesaws to catapult cubes across the screen or trigger chain reactions.
  • Versatile Modes: Features handcrafted Puzzles, an Endless mode, and split-screen local PvP (with Steam Remote Play Together support).
  • Level Editor & Steam Workshop: Full in-game toolset to build and share custom levels with the community.
